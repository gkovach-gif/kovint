When you hire Kovint, you work with me directly.

Services

Six ways I help products get made

Each service maps to where a product sits on the Technology Readiness Level (TRL) scale, from early research to factory launch.

TRL 1–9

Innovation Strategy & Roadmaps

Build an innovation pipeline you can defend and actually deliver.

  • TRL stage-gate process design
  • Cross-functional roadmap workshops
  • Team structure, budget and headcount planning
TRL 3–7

Footwear Development & Performance

Development and biomechanics support for new and existing footwear.

  • Bottom-unit material characterization
  • Mechanical test methods and standards
  • Fit, cushioning and performance validation
TRL 2–8

Connected Products & Sensors

Put electronics into footwear, apparel and wearables without hurting comfort or durability.

  • Sensor selection, placement and integration
  • Algorithm, ML and data-analysis development
  • Launched Under Armour's first smart shoes
TRL 2–6

Prototyping & Design Development

Go from sketch to test-ready prototypes quickly.

  • 3D CAD: SolidWorks, NX, Creo, Rhino, Onshape
  • FEA and simulation in Ansys and Abaqus
  • 3D printing and in-house prototype lab setup
TRL 1–6

Research Labs & Testing Programs

Design and build testing capability that produces data teams can use.

  • Lab strategy, equipment specification and build-out
  • Mechanical, physiology and perception protocols
  • Athlete and consumer insight studies
TRL 6–9

Design-to-Manufacturing & Quality

Keep the design intent intact through the move to mass production.

  • Factory transfer with partners in Asia and Europe
  • End-of-line QC stations and multi-stage QC systems
  • Tooling, sourcing and material standards
